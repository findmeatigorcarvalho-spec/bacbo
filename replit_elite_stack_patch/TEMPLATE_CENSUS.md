# Template census — day 1 → today (Vany corpus)

Source scan: `timing_presence_catalog.json` (535,697 messages, Mar 19 → Jul 18 2026 era in export).

## Three different ways to count “how many types”

| Level | What it means | Count |
|-------|----------------|------:|
| **A. Structural skins** | Distinct card layouts (numbers/rooms stripped fingerprints) | **19,081** |
| **B. Named signal kinds** | Engine/tier labels on fires+results | **~4–8 core** (+ ops) |
| **C. Semantic families** | Human-readable fire/result buckets | **~10 fire · ~8–10 result** |

Skins ≫ families: same family (e.g. SOLO ELITE ENTER NOW) has many skin variants over time.

### Message volume (role)
| Role | Messages |

WITH_TIMING skins **4,225** · NO_TIMING skins **14,856**.

## B) Signal kinds in general (core)

Primary kinds seen across system + cards:

1. `SOLO_ELITE`  
2. `GOLDEN`  
3. `SEQUENCE`  
4. `PLATINUM`  
5. `FLASH` (camada/flash windows)  
6. Countdown / Clock-A / `CD_*` timer family  
7. `ORACLE` result skin  
8. Gale/ops (`G1 EXPIROU`, `G2 MISS`, gale follow-ups)

Luxury `kinds_bias`: SOLO_ELITE · SEQUENCE · GOLDEN · PLATINUM.

## Short answer

- **~19k** different card **skins** ever  
- **~10 fire families** + **~8–10 result families** worth treating as types  
- **~4 main signal kinds** (SOLO / GOLDEN / SEQUENCE / PLATINUM) plus flash/countdown/ops  

#6–#10 museum batch = mostly **SOLO_ELITE** fire + **SHORT_WIN** result, with one **GOLDEN** fire + **SHORT_LOSS** — same early generation, not one identical type.
